# SF004.4: LLM Match Assessment

## Subfeature Overview
The LLM Match Assessment subfeature enhances job matching with AI-powered analysis to provide qualitative assessments of match quality. It leverages large language models to analyze both candidate skills and job requirements, generating explanations of the match quality and identifying key strengths and potential gaps.

### Assessment Approach
For the MVP, the system will implement a single-stage LLM analysis that:
1. Receives candidate information and job details that pass the initial scoring threshold
2. Analyzes the match quality based on skills, experience, and preferences
3. Provides a comprehensive analysis of how the job relates to the candidate's profile
4. Makes a final binary determination (Match/No Match) based on qualitative analysis
5. Explains the reasoning for the match or non-match determination

### LLM Prompt Structure
The following structure should be used for prompting the LLM:

```
You are analyzing a job posting for {Company} for the role of {JobTitle} to determine how well it matches a candidate's profile.

CANDIDATE PROFILE:
Skills: {CandidateSkills}
Experience: {CandidateExperience}
Preferences: {CandidatePreferences}

JOB DETAILS:
Title: {JobTitle}
Company: {Company}
Description: {JobDescription}
Requirements: {JobRequirements}
Location: {JobLocation}
Additional Info: {JobAdditionalInfo}

INITIAL MATCH METRICS:
Initial Score: {RawScore}
Match Percentage: {MatchPercentage}%
Match Threshold: 30%

TASK:
First, analyze the job posting against the candidate's profile by considering the following factors:
1. Skill alignment - how well do the candidate's skills match the job requirements?
2. Experience match - does the candidate have relevant experience for this role?
3. Location compatibility - does the job location match the candidate's preferences?
4. Career trajectory - does this job make sense for the candidate's career path?
5. Industry alignment - is this in an industry aligned with the candidate's background?

Write a comprehensive analysis examining these factors and explaining how well the job matches the candidate's profile. Include specific examples of alignments and misalignments.

Based on your thorough analysis above, provide:
1. A concise summary of the match quality (2-3 sentences)
2. A list of 3-5 specific matching points between the candidate and the job
3. A list of 1-3 potential gaps or misalignments
4. Your final determination: Is this job a MATCH or NO MATCH for this candidate?
5. A brief explanation of your final determination

Your analysis should be objective, thorough, and educational, focusing on qualitative reasoning rather than numerical scoring.
```

### LLM Output Requirements
The system must parse the LLM's response to extract:
- The comprehensive match analysis (`job_match_analysis` field)
- The concise match summary (`job_match_summary` field)
- The final match determination (`is_match` field)

### Non-Matching Job Handling
For jobs determined to be non-matches by the LLM:
- Store detailed analysis of why the job doesn't match in `job_match_analysis`
- Store concise summary of the non-match reasoning in `job_match_summary`
- Set `is_match` to FALSE
- Ensure these non-matches are still accessible to users through a "Non-Matches" section
