I want to change this prompt to be more specific to software developers.

To best find a job for software developers we need to know:

- What languages they know and prefer
- What frameworks they know and prefer
- What databases they know and prefer
- What tools they know and prefer
- What platforms they know and prefer
- What cloud platforms they know and prefer

For each subject above, we also need to know what they don't know. For instance
if they mention 'Python' and 'Django' but not 'Flask' or 'FastAPI' then we need
to know that. Maybe they don't know it or don't prefer it but we need to be very specific.

Another example, if they didn't mention any front end related subjects we need to know
if they don't know or want to work on front end technologies.

This is true for all the subjects above.  Did they not mention Docker? Ask about it.
Did they not mention Kubernetes? Ask about it. Did they not mention AWS? Ask about it.
