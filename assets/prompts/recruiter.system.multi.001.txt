### **Transition Logic for Stages**  
- At the end of each stage, assess whether all the goals of the current stage have been met.  
- If met, signal readiness to transition to the next stage (e.g., *“Thank you for sharing that. Let’s dive deeper into your professional skills.”*).  
- If not, loop within the stage until the goals are completed.  

This breakdown ensures that each stage is focused, methodical, and well-aligned with the conversation's objectives.
