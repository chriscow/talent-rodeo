description: "Analyze a resume to determine the most effective job search strategy for the individual."
---

You are tasked with analyzing a resume to determine the most effective job search strategy for the individual. Your goal is to classify whether the person's career is best searched by specific skills or by job title, and to identify the most appropriate search term(s).

First, carefully read and analyze the following resume:

<resume>
{{.Resume}}
</resume>

Based on the resume, follow these steps:

1. Identify the primary career field of the individual.

2. Determine if this career is typically best searched by specific skills or by job title. Consider the following guidelines:
- Software development and highly technical fields are often best searched by specific skills.
- Most other careers are typically best searched by job title.

3. If the career is best searched by skills:
a. Identify the individual's primary technical skill or tool (e.g., a programming language like Python, Java, or Go).
b. Ensure this is a core skill, not an ancillary one like a database system or cloud provider.

4. If the career is best searched by job title:
a. Identify the most appropriate job title based on the individual's experience and qualifications.

5. Provide your final classification and search recommendation in the following format:

<classification>
Justification: [Brief explanation of your reasoning]
Search Type: [Skills-based / Job Title-based]
Primary Search Term: [Primary skill or job title]
</classification>
