Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 31 May 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Talent Rodeo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered job matching platform.

Talent Rodeo (referred to as "we," "us," or "our") operates a two-sided marketplace that helps job seekers find opportunities aligned with their skills, experience, and preferences while enabling employers to discover qualified candidates through AI-driven conversations, intelligent job matching, and AI-facilitated introductions.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Job Seeker Information

When you register as a job seeker, we collect the following types of personal information:

  • Account Information: When you register using LinkedIn OAuth, we collect your name, email address, and LinkedIn profile information.
  • Resume Data: Information contained in resumes you upload, including your work history, skills, education, and other professional details.
  • Contact Information: Your phone number for verification and calls with our AI.
  • Communication Records: Recordings and transcripts of calls used by our AI to match you to jobs.
  • Usage Information: How you interact with our platform, including features used and job matches reviewed.
  • Subscription Data: Information related to your service tier and, if applicable, payment information.

2.2 Employer/Company Information

When you register as an employer or company representative, we collect:

  • Company Account Information: Company name, industry, size, and authorized representative details.
  • Contact Information: Business email address, phone number, and office location.
  • Job Posting Data: Job descriptions, requirements, compensation ranges, and hiring preferences.
  • Search Criteria: Skills, experience levels, and candidate preferences for matching purposes.
  • Billing Information: Payment method details and subscription information for employer services.
  • Usage Information: How you interact with candidate profiles and introduction requests.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you visit or use our platform:

  • Device Information: Type of device, operating system, browser type, and internet connection.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, pages viewed, and activities on our platform.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: Information collected through cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

3.1 For Job Seekers

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To analyze your skills and experience for job matching
  • To conduct assessment calls and extract relevant information
  • To search for and recommend job opportunities
  • To create aggregate, anonymized summaries of your qualifications for employer matching (with your consent)
  • To facilitate introductions with interested employers (only with your explicit consent)
  • To manage your subscription and provide appropriate features
  • To communicate with you about your account and potential opportunities

3.2 For Employers

  • To create and maintain your company account
  • To process and display your job postings
  • To enable candidate search and discovery features
  • To provide aggregate candidate summaries based on your search criteria
  • To facilitate introductions with interested candidates
  • To process payments and manage your subscription
  • To communicate with you about your account and candidate matches

3.3 Platform Operations

  • To ensure the security and functionality of our platform
  • To improve our services and develop new features
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and for legitimate business purposes:

  • Job Seekers: Your resume and LinkedIn profile are only used during calls with our AI to derive your search criteria and qualifications summary.
  • Inactive Accounts: If you cease to interact with our system (either through website login or phone conversations with our AI), we will delete your data after 60 days of inactivity.
  • Employer Data: Company information and job postings are retained for the duration of your subscription and for 30 days after cancellation.
  • User-Requested Deletion: You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
  • Introduction Records: We maintain records of successful introductions for 12 months for quality assurance and dispute resolution purposes.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information. Period.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. Your contact information, resume details, and personal identifiers are never sold or shared without your explicit consent.

5.2 Aggregate Candidate Summaries

We may share aggregate, anonymized summaries of candidate qualifications with employer subscribers for matching purposes. These summaries:

  • Do not include personally identifiable information such as names, contact details, current employer names, or specific company references
  • Focus on skills, experience levels, education, and general career background
  • Are created only for candidates who have opted into our introduction service
  • Are used solely to help employers identify potential matches before requesting an introduction

5.3 Introduction Service

Our AI-facilitated introduction service operates as follows:

  • Candidate Consent Required: When our AI identifies a potential match, we contact candidates first to gauge interest before any introduction
  • Voluntary Participation: Candidates always have the right to decline an introduction with no impact on other services
  • Controlled Information Sharing: Only after candidate consent do we provide contact information to enable direct communication between parties
  • Introduction Details: We share the candidate's name, contact information, and professional summary with the employer only after explicit consent
  • Mutual Introduction: We simultaneously share the employer's company information and job details with the candidate

5.4 Service Providers

We may share information with service providers who help us deliver our services (e.g., hosting, data analysis, customer service, payment processing). These providers are contractually obligated to use your information only for providing services to us and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

5.5 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or the rights, property, or safety of others.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data is fully encrypted in transit and at rest following well-established best practices for security and privacy
  • Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security assessments and updates to ensure ongoing protection
  • Secure authentication mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access
  • Separate security protocols for job seeker and employer data

7. Data Storage Location

All user data is stored in the United States.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing of your information
  • Data Portability: Request transfer of your information to another service
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing
  • Introduction Opt-Out: Withdraw consent for the introduction service at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 13.

9. Consent and Communication Preferences

9.1 Job Seeker Consent

  • Call Recording: By using our service, you consent to the recording of assessment calls for AI analysis and matching purposes
  • Introduction Service: You may opt into our introduction service to allow employers to request connections based on aggregate summaries
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time, though this may limit our ability to provide certain services

9.2 Employer Communication

  • Candidate Contact: Employers may only contact candidates through our introduction service
  • Direct Contact Prohibition: Employers are prohibited from using our service to obtain candidate contact information for direct solicitation

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child, we will take steps to delete that information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice through email or platform notifications. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

12. Employer Responsibilities

Employers using our platform agree to:

  • Use candidate information only for legitimate hiring purposes
  • Respect candidate privacy and communication preferences
  • Not attempt to circumvent our introduction service to contact candidates directly
  • Comply with applicable employment and privacy laws
  • Report any misuse of the platform or candidate information

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@talent.rodeo
  • General Support: support@talent.rodeo

14. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (which we do not engage in). For more information about your California privacy rights, please contact us.

15. International Users

If you are accessing our services from outside the United States, please be aware that your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States. By using our services, you consent to this transfer, storing, and processing of your information in the United States.
