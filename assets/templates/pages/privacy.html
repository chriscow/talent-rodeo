Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 18 April 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Talent Rodeo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered job matching platform.

Talent Rodeo (referred to as "we," "us," or "our") provides a platform that helps job seekers find opportunities aligned with their skills, experience, and preferences through AI-driven conversations, intelligent job matching, and personalized document optimization.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following types of personal information:

  • Account Information: When you register using LinkedIn OAuth, we collect your name, email address, and LinkedIn profile information.
  • Resume Data: Information contained in resumes you upload, including your work history, skills, education, and other professional details.
  • Contact Information: Your phone number for verification and calls with our AI.
  • Communication Records: Recordings and transcripts of calls used by our AI to match you to jobs.
  • Usage Information: How you interact with our platform, including features used and job matches reviewed.
  • Subscription Data: Information related to your service tier and, if applicable, payment information.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We automatically collect certain information when you visit or use our platform:

  • Device Information: Type of device, operating system, browser type, and internet connection.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, pages viewed, and activities on our platform.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: Information collected through cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for the purpose of providing and improving our services:

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To analyze your skills and experience for job matching
  • To conduct assessment calls and extract relevant information
  • To search for and recommend job opportunities
  • To customize documents for specific job applications
  • To manage your subscription and provide tier-appropriate features
  • To ensure the security and functionality of our platform
  • To communicate with you about your account and our services

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide our services and for legitimate business purposes:

  • We do not store your resume. It's contents are only used for the call with our AI to derive your search criteria.
  • If you cease to interact with our system (either through website login or phone conversations with our AI), we will delete your data after 60 days of inactivity.
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

5.1 We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information. Period.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5.2 Limited Sharing

The only data shared with any third parties is derived and unidentifiable data, specifically:

  • Derived search queries based on your preferences for job matching purposes
  • Aggregated, anonymized data for service improvement and analysis

5.3 Service Providers

We may share information with service providers who help us deliver our services (e.g., hosting, data analysis, customer service). These providers are contractually obligated to use your information only for providing services to us and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

5.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, legal process, or government request, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or the rights, property, or safety of others.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data is fully encrypted in transit and at rest following well-established best practices for security and privacy
  • Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • Regular security assessments and updates to ensure ongoing protection
  • Secure authentication mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access

7. Data Storage Location

All user data is stored in the United States.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing of your information
  • Data Portability: Request transfer of your information to another service
  • Objection: Object to certain types of processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 12.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child, we will take steps to delete that information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

11. Consent to Call Recording

By using our service, you consent to the recording of assessment calls. You may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us, though this may limit our ability to provide certain services.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: support@talent.rodeo

13. California Privacy Rights

California residents may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). For more information, please contact us.

14. International Users

If you are accessing our services from outside the United States, please be aware that your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States. By using our services, you consent to this transfer, storing, and processing of your information in the United States.
