The Talent Rodeo Story

Talent Rodeo was born from a simple frustration: job hunting is exhausting, time-consuming, and often disappointing.

As a software developer with decades of experience (including time at Microsoft during the browser wars), I found myself dreading the job search process. Reading through countless job descriptions only to discover most weren't a good fit was wasting precious time and energy.

Having worked with AI technologies for the past couple of years, I realized there was a better way. What started as a tool to solve my own problem evolved into Talent Rodeo — a platform designed to help others navigate the job market more efficiently.

Founded in early 2025, Talent Rodeo represents my commitment to creating software that truly serves its users.

What Makes Talent Rodeo Different

Most job platforms only analyze your resume and online forms. Your resume only describes the past, not your future. Talent Rodeo takes a more personal approach.

Our service actually calls you on the phone, having a real conversation to understand what you're looking for in your next role. This analog connection helps us gather nuances that digital forms simply can't capture.

Is it perfect? Not yet — but we're continuously improving to provide the most human-centered job matching experience possible.
